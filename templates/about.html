About Under an Aspect

This is a site where I write about books and ideas: literary criticism, long-form philosophical essays, and other kinds of analytical writing.

The name recalls something that happened when I was looking down on a pine forest in the Maritime Alps. I saw a patch of luminous yellow birch trees standing out among the dark conifers, until a perceptual switch flipped and I saw them for what they really were: pine trees like all the rest, lit by the evening sun through a gap in the hills. Nothing about the trees had changed. I had simply come to see them under a different aspect, and I couldn't get the first one back. Books do this too. Most of what I write here is an attempt to work out which aspect of a work I’m seeing, or which aspect is best—and I don’t mind changing my mind halfway through a piece, to force a change of aspect.

When I’m not writing here I run The Philosophy Forum, an online community I started in 2015 and have been running ever since. It's one of the best places online for philosophical discussion, and I'm still taking an active part in the community: moderating, organizing reading groups, and keeping things going behind the scenes.

I’m also a software developer. I've built several web applications, including CommissionCrowd, which I co-founded.

If you would like to get the articles by email as they appear, you can subscribe at the bottom of any post.
